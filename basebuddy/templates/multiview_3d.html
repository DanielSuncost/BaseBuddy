info About Multiview 3D Reconstruction

help_outline What is this?

Create 3D models of your plants using multiple synchronized camera views. Perfect for tracking growth in three dimensions!

checklist Requirements

  • Cameras: Minimum 2, recommended 4 cameras viewing the same plant
  • Timelapse: Enable timelapse capture so cameras have synchronized images
  • Masks (optional): Create segmentation masks to focus on plant regions

rocket_launch Reconstruction Engines

Modern feed-forward engines reconstruct dense point clouds from a handful of views with no calibration:

  • VGGT: Feed-forward transformer, fast and dense. Recommended when installed.
  • π³ (Pi3): Permutation-equivariant feed-forward reconstruction.
  • DUSt3R: Dense unconstrained stereo reconstruction, works with just 2 cameras.
  • SfM: Classic feature matching + auto-calibration fallback, no ML models required.
  • Auto: Picks the best available engine automatically.

Engines that are not installed appear disabled in the engine selector.

trending_up Growth Tracking Workflow

  • Create segmentation masks so reconstructions focus on the plant
  • Run a 3D reconstruction from the Cameras tab
  • Open it in the 3D viewer and set a real-world scale (measure a known object, e.g. the pot width)
  • Repeat over days — the Growth tab charts height and volume over time

crop Registration Regions

Burned-in text (timestamps, camera-name banners, logos) looks identical across views and gets falsely matched as shared 3D points. Use the "Regions" button on a camera card to fix this:

  • Exclude boxes (red) are never used for registration — draw them over timestamps and logos
  • Include boxes (green): if any exist, only those areas are used
  • Optionally restrict registration to the camera's saved segmentation mask
  • Exclude boxes always apply, even when "Use plant masks" is unchecked

masks About Segmentation Masks

Masks help the system focus on the plant and ignore background:

  • Click the "Mask" button on any camera card to create/edit a mask
  • Left-click on the plant (green = foreground)
  • Right-click on background (red = exclude)
  • Masks are shown overlaid on camera previews
  • Matching is constrained to masked regions for better accuracy
